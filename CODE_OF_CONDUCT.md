# Code of conduct

Scrapbook/01 is a small project, and we'd like it to be a pleasant place to work on.

## The rules

1. Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
3. Don't share anyone's personal information, including the saves in their library.
4. Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## What the maintainers may do

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people
from the repository when these rules are broken. For a first, minor problem that usually starts with
a friendly note.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/franco-roura/scrapbook-01/security/advisories/new)
(say it's a conduct report) or by contacting a maintainer on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential.
